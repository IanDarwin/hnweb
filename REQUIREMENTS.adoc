[#intro]
== Introduction

=== Purpose

`hnweb` is a web application that lets the executive of a small non-profit nature club keep track of
its membership: who belongs, what type of membership they have, when it expires, who has renewed and
how they paid, and how to contact them. It also publishes the club's upcoming meetings and lets
authorized people send e-mail to groups of members.

It was built for the Headwaters Nature club (Orangeville, Ontario, Canada), is configured by an
organization name, and is described in its README as a sample of Jakarta Persistence in a JSF environment.
The TODO list includes generalizing it for use by other clubs.

=== Scope

In scope:

* Member records, including family memberships and an archive ("deleted") state.
* Login, role-based access, self-service password set/reset.
* Annual membership renewal and a log of renewal transactions.
* Meeting/event listing and administration.
* Bulk e-mail to groups of members, with an optional attachment.
* CSV/text extracts of the member list; name-badge preparation (stub).
* A public-facing signup form with a small CGI backend.
* Operator scripts for reporting and maintenance against the database.

Out of scope (not implemented at present): online payment, a self-service member profile editor,
a public signup flow that writes to the database, 
and possibly multi-club (multi-tenant) operation.

=== Definitions

[horizontal]
Member:: A person with a row in the `Member` table whose discriminator `type` is `Member` (active).
Archived member:: A row whose `type` is `MemberArchived`. It is the same row, with the type flipped; see <<fr-archive>>.
Family master:: The `Family` (FM) member who pays for the family. Secondary members point at the master.
Secondary:: A `Secondary` (FS) member: an extra person on a family membership.
Membership year:: The year from 1 September to 31 August, named for the calendar year in which it _ends_ (September 2026 to August 2027 is "2027").
Expiry year:: The membership year through which a member is paid up. `0` means "never paid".
Executive ("exec"):: A member whose `position` field is non-empty (president, treasurer, and so on).
Admin:: A member with the `admin` role in the `member_roles` table.

== Overall description

=== Product perspective

A server-side Jakarta EE web application (WAR) deployed to a Jakarta EE application server (WildFly in the README).
Pages are Jakarta Faces (JSF) Facelets with PrimeFaces 13; persistence is JPA with Hibernate against a relational
database (PostgreSQL in operation, per the scripts and the driver in the build; in-memory H2 in tests).

[source]
----
                    +------------------------+
 Browser ---HTTPS-->| hnweb WAR (WildFly)    |-----JDBC------> database ("hn")
 (members,          |  JSF pages  *.web      |
  executives,       |  PageFilter (auth)     |-----SMTP------> mail server
  admins)           |  JAX-RS /api/...       |                 (java:jboss/mail/Default)
                    +------------------------+
 Browser ---POST /signup---> membership-mailer (separate C/kcgi CGI) ---SMTP---> club admin
 Operator ---ssh/psql---> scripts/*  (reports, bulk fixes)
----

=== Operating environment and constraints

* Jakarta EE 11 API, Java 17 or later, JSF/Facelets with PrimeFaces 13 (theme "saga"), Hibernate ORM 7.
* Application server supplies the JTA datasource `java:jboss/datasources/HnClubDataSource` and the mail session `java:jboss/mail/Default`.
* All pages are served through the Faces servlet mapped to `*.web`; request and response encoding is UTF-8.
* `config.properties` (on the classpath) supplies the organization name, mail settings and the renewal month.
* The membership year is fixed at 1 September to 31 August by default; the renewal month is a property (`datetime.renewalmonth`, default 9).
* Canadian context: provinces are the Canadian provinces and territories; amounts on the signup form are in Canadian dollars.

=== Assumptions

* An e-mail address uniquely identifies a member for the purposes of login and password reset (nothing enforces this; see <<gap-email-unique>>).
* The club is small enough (hundreds of members) that lists are loaded whole and filtered in memory, and tables are not paged.
* Executives and admins are trusted with all members' personal data.

[#business]
== Business rules

[#br-year]
=== Membership year

[horizontal]
BR-01:: The membership year runs 1 September to 31 August and is named for the year it ends. In or after the renewal month (default September) the current membership year is the _next_ calendar year; before it, the current calendar year.
BR-02:: A member joining today has an expiry year equal to the current membership year, except that someone joining in or after the renewal month gets `year + 1` (the same thing, restated in `getNewMemberExpiryYear`).
BR-03:: A member is *expired* when the calendar year is past their expiry year, or when it is the expiry year and the month is after the renewal month (so there is a one-month grace period: a member with expiry year 2026 is still current through September 2026). A secondary is expired when their master is.

[#br-renew]
=== Renewal

[horizontal]
BR-04:: A renewal of _n_ years sets the new expiry year to `max(current expiry year, current membership year - 1) + n`.
BR-05:: Consequence: a member who has lapsed gets one year bought as "the current membership year", not as an extension from their old expiry (for example, a member who expired in 2026, renewing for one year in October 2026, is paid through 2027); a member who is paid ahead keeps the years they have already paid.

.Worked examples (from `YearCalcsTest`)
[cols="1,1,1,1,1"]
|===
|Today |Expiry before |Years |Expiry after |Why

|2026-10 |2026 |1 |2027 |lapsed: current year is 2027
|2026-10 |2020 |1 |2027 |long lapsed: starts from now
|2026-10 |2026 |2 |2028 |
|2026-10 |2027 |1 |2028 |paid ahead: extend
|2026-10 |2029 |1 |2030 |paid ahead: keep extra years
|2026-03 |2025 |1 |2026 |before September the current year is 2026
|===

[horizontal]
BR-06:: Payments are cash, cheque or Interac e-transfer at renewal; the system also knows credit card, debit card and unknown. Credit card numbers are never stored.
BR-07:: A person is either an Individual, Family master, Family Secondary, Student, or Mail-Only (not a member; on the mailing list only). Expiry is not meaningful for Mail-Only people (not enforced; with expiry 0 they are never "paid-up").

[#br-mail]
=== Who gets which mailing

[cols="1,4"]
|===
|Mailing |Recipients

|Test |The sender only.
|Executive |Active members with a non-empty `position`.
|Paid-up |Active members whose expiry year's 31 August is still in the future.
|Entire list |Every active member with an e-mail address, current and former.
|Expired ("nags") |Active members whose expiry year is before the current calendar year.
|===

NOTE: The "paid-up" and "expired" rules use different definitions of the year. See <<gap-mail-rules>>.

[#br-access]
=== Access and privacy

[horizontal]
BR-08:: Only executives and admins may see other members' personal information (the Privacy page promises this).
BR-09:: Only admins may change data (create, edit, archive, renew, events, transactions). Executives are read-only, but see <<gap-undelete-role>>.
BR-10:: The club stores name, city, e-mail and phone numbers. It states that it no longer stores street addresses and never stores credit card numbers.
BR-11:: Passwords are stored as hashes, never in the clear.

=== Data rules

[horizontal]
DR-01:: An archived member keeps every field, including the link to their master, so restoring is lossless.
DR-02:: Member identity is the database id. Two member objects are equal only if both have an id and the ids match.
DR-03:: Only `Member` rows (never archived rows) carry roles and master/secondary links in the object model.
DR-04:: Dates are stored without time zone; event times are stored as text in "HH:mm" form.
DR-05:: The schema is created and updated automatically at deployment (`schema-generation: update`), so a new deployment needs no hand-run DDL after the first run.

[#interfaces]
== External interfaces

[horizontal]
IR-01:: *Database.* A JTA datasource named `HnClubDataSource`. Hibernate is the JPA provider. Tests use in-memory H2.
IR-02:: *Mail.* The Jakarta Mail session `java:jboss/mail/Default`. Properties in `config.properties`: `mail.smtp.host`, `mail.smtp.from` (no-reply address), `mail.subject.prefix`, `mail.unsubscribe`.
IR-03:: *REST.* `POST /api/changePassword1` (form field `email`) and `POST /api/changePassword2` (form fields `token`, `newPassword`). Both answer with an HTTP 303 redirect to a `.web` page; there are no JSON endpoints.
IR-04:: *Signup CGI.* `POST /signup` handled by the separate C program `membership-mailer` (kcgi), which e-mails the form fields to the club administrator.
IR-05:: *Configuration.* `config.properties` (organization name `orgName`, mail properties, `datetime.renewalmonth`).
IR-06:: *Operator scripts.* Shell and awk scripts in `scripts/` talk to the PostgreSQL database `hn` directly with `psql` (see <<ops>>).

[#nfr]
== Non-functional requirements

[horizontal]
NFR-01 (Security):: All data-changing and personal-data pages require login and a role (FR-06). Session timeout is 2 hours. Password reset uses unguessable single-use tokens (FR-14) and a uniform error for every failure (FR-15).
NFR-02 (Security):: Passwords are stored as unsalted MD5. See <<gap-md5>>.
NFR-03 (Privacy):: Credit card data is never stored; the Privacy page states what is kept and who sees it.
NFR-04 (Usability):: A single modern, responsive layout (stylesheet `css/stylesheet.css`); forms show inline field messages; destructive actions ask for confirmation; the password page gives live feedback.
NFR-05 (Internationalization):: Text is English only. Character encoding is UTF-8 end to end (request, response, and outgoing mail).
NFR-06 (Deployability):: One WAR plus a datasource, a mail session and `config.properties`. README steps are acknowledged to be "nowhere near user-friendly". Moving to a stand-alone Quarkus service on port 8500 is a stated goal (`TODO.txt`).
NFR-07 (Maintainability):: Per-page access rules live in small `page.xml` files rather than in code; business rules for dates are isolated in `YearCalcs`.
NFR-08 (Testability):: Unit and integration tests run against H2 with no server: year calculations, login, member DAO, mail building and sanitizing, page-restriction parsing, the full password-reset flow (with an in-JVM SMTP server), and a well-formed-XML check on pages.
NFR-09 (Performance):: No stated target. Lists are loaded in full and filtered in memory.
NFR-10 (Availability / data safety):: No explicit requirement. Archiving rather than deleting is the main safeguard against accidental loss.

[#gaps]
== Known gaps, inconsistencies and open questions

This section lists places where the code contradicts itself, its own documentation, or an evident intent.
Each is based on reading the source; none was reproduced by running the application. They are ordered roughly by
how much they matter. Items marked *Verify* are the most important to check against a running system.

=== Security and privacy


[#gap-xhtml-direct]
Direct `.xhtml` requests (*Verify*)::
The filter applies its rules only to URLs ending `.web`. If the container also serves or Faces-processes the same pages under their `.xhtml` names, those requests skip the access rules. Mapping the Faces servlet to `*.web` only, and disabling automatic `.xhtml` mapping, is the usual defence.

[#gap-md5]
Password hashing::
Passwords are unsalted MD5. The Privacy page says they would be "very difficult to deconstruct (as of 2020)", which is no longer a safe claim. A salted, slow hash (Argon2, bcrypt or PBKDF2) with migration on next login is the standard replacement. `scripts/passwd` and `LoginAction` both depend on the MD5 scheme.

[#gap-undelete-role]
Restoring is not admin-only::
`TODO.txt` says "Ensure Resurrected (and all data-changing) requires admin". `DeletedList` is open to executives and the Undelete button is not role-checked, so an executive can restore members.

[#gap-memberview]
`MemberView` restriction timing (*Verify*)::
The rule "the member themself" compares the logged-in user with `memberHome.instance`, but `PageFilter` evaluates it before the page's view action has loaded that member. A plain member may therefore be refused their own page, or `memberHome.instance` may be some earlier user's record held in the conversation.

[#gap-roles]
Role names::
README says `club_exec`; the code recognizes `admin` and a synthetic `exec` (from the position field).

[#gap-email-unique]
E-mail is not unique::
Login and password reset both assume one member per e-mail address (and fail, or report "no member", if there are two) but nothing in the model or the edit form enforces it.

=== Functional defects

[#gap-event-times]
Event times::
`MeetingEventProducer` stores times as "1900" and "2100", but `Event.getStartDateTime()` splits on ":" and falls back to 12:00 with a `printf` that has a `%s` but no argument (which throws). Generated meetings also leave the free-text `times` field (shown on the home page) empty. The producer's comment says regular meetings are on the "3rd Tuesday" while the code passes `RecurringEventDatePicker.LAST`.

[#gap-schedule]
Calendar page::
`ScheduleBean` reads upcoming events from an injected DAO inside its constructor, when the injection has not happened yet, and it labels every event "Monthly Meeting".
It is unfinished.

[#gap-past-events]
Past events::
The event list shows only upcoming events; there is no way to see a past event.

[#gap-new-tx]
Initial transaction::
Every new member, including mailing-list-only and secondary members, gets a "new" transaction for one year with no payment type and no resulting expiry year, which will look like a payment in the transaction log.

[#gap-mail-rules]
Mail recipient rules::
* The "paid-up" rule uses the membership year (expiry year's 31 August); the "expired" rule uses the calendar year (expiry year before this calendar year). A member whose expiry year is this year, in October or later, is in neither group, and the date used by the paid-up rule is captured when the class is first loaded (`static LocalDate now`), so it goes stale on a long-running server.
* Secondaries are judged on their own expiry year, not their master's, unlike the member list and `YearCalcs`.

[#gap-hardcoded]
Hard-coded club details::
The mail's `List-Id` and `List-Unsubscribe` headers, the password-reset site address (`https://hn.darwinsys.com`), the reset subject ("HN Passord Reset", sic), the "Monthly Meeting" label, menu links and the signup form's address and fees are fixed in code or pages, which conflicts with the aim of reuse by other clubs.

[#gap-newmember-rule]
"After March 1" rule not implemented::
The signup form says "New memberships after March 1 extend to the following year", and `Config` defines a constant for it,
but it is unused and misnamed; a member joining in April 2027 gets expiry 2027 (this membership year), not 2028.

[#gap-pages]
Pages that do not work or are incomplete::
`ClubStats` (missing bean), `Exec` (calls a missing method), `Schedule` (unfinished), `BadgePrint` (nothing happens), `MemberTransactionEdit` (raw text fields for enums and the member), "Add DeletedView" (a missing page, in `TODO.txt`).

[#gap-family]
Family handling still open::
CSV and member list do not show a secondary's master consistently (`TODO.txt`: "show master when displaying a secondary").

[#gap-privacy-address]
Street address::
The Privacy page says street addresses are no longer stored, but the entity still has `address`, `address2` and `postcode` columns and `MemberHome` still contains a postal-code validator that no page uses.

[#gap-scripts]
Stale scripts::
`add_secondary` uses a `member_type` column name that differs from the mapped `membertype`.

=== Open questions for the owner

. Should executives be allowed to restore archived members, or only admins (FR-42, BR-09)?
. Is the one-month grace period (BR-03) intended, and should "paid-up" and "expired" mailings use it?
. Should a new member who joins after 1 March be paid to the _following_ year (the form says so; the code does not)?
. Should a family renewal automatically carry the secondaries (FR-52), or is "follow the master" enough?
. Which of the stub pages (stats, calendar, badges) are still wanted?
. Is members editing their own record, and online renewal and payment (Helcim, in `TODO.txt`), a firm goal? That changes the security model (members would need write access to their own row only).
. What is the intended retention of archived people, and should they ever be purged?
. Is multi-club use a goal? If so, club name, mail headers, host name and fees need to become configuration.

Mailing List

This gives the ability to generate a mass mailing to the club membership.

Just fill in the subject, and enter your message, using the controls to add emphasis, links, and so on, rather as you would in a word processor or fancier email client. We strongly recommend that you use the "TEST" button before sending to the group, to make sure that the message is going to look the way you want to be represented (it also gives you a minute or so to cool off in the unlikely event that tempers flare...).

Note that the email will appear to come from your name at the email address in your profile, and that you will get a (probably duplicate) bcc copy sent to your email.

Mail a short note Subject: Message: Add-Attachment: N.B.: To attach a pdf or jpg/png, you must use both Choose and Upload buttons. Files limited to 5 MB. You must log out after sending a message to the list, otherwise problems may occur, like old attachments getting sent with new messages.
Also note: at present, it's limited to one attachment per email; I can extend this if it's needed.
